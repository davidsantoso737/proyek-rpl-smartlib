# Audit 005 — Full-width shell with content whitespace (2026-10-10)

Scope: librarian outer shell padding vs inner content padding. Inspected
`base.html` shell (lines 20-27), aside, header bar, header inner row,
`main`. Student/teacher branch untouched by design.

## Spacing audit (verified)

Three responsibilities are currently conflated in one shell container
`max-w-[1400px] px-4 sm:px-6 lg:px-8`: viewport coverage, sidebar
position, and content inset. Consequences: sidebar starts 32px off the
left edge and the header bar ends 32px before the right edge at
desktop, while content has no padding of its own and would collapse
onto the seam if the shell padding were simply deleted.

## Approved fix (per task directive)

- Shell: `w-full px-4 sm:px-6 lg:px-0` (viewport coverage only; 16px
  mobile and 24px tablet inset preserved, zero at desktop).
- Main: add `lg:px-8` (32px inner whitespace both sides; vertical
  `py-6 sm:py-8` unchanged).
- Header inner row: add `lg:px-8` so controls keep 32px from the right
  edge while the bar surface spans seam to viewport edge.
- Aside `rounded-b-xl` to `rounded-br-xl` (square bottom-left at the
  viewport edge); header `lg:rounded-bl-none` to `lg:rounded-none`
  (square meets seam and right edge). No color, shadow, icon, content,
  or behavior changes.
