# Audit 003 — Librarian top alignment, header surface, footer (2026-10-10)

Scope: shared librarian shell geometry plus footer. Inspected `base.html`
librarian shell, `_librarian_sidebar.html`, `_brand.html`, and title
blocks of dashboard, approval_list, ebook_approval_list, handover,
return, return_history, manage_book_list, opname_list, report,
user_list, policy, material_review_list, notification_list. No new
screenshots supplied; bookhive.jpg inspected in audit 001.

## Root cause (verified, not assumed)

All librarian routes share one shell and all title blocks use `mb-6`,
so no page-specific wrapper causes the strip. The inconsistency is
scroll-state dependent behavior inside the shared shell itself:

1. [HIGH] Sidebar sticks at `top-6` while the workspace header sticks at
    `top-0` with a transparent slate-tinted background. Short pages that
    never scroll (typical Sirkulasi form pages) rest aligned at y=0 and
    look correct; long scrolling pages (dashboard, manage lists, reports)
    end up with a 24px empty strip above the stuck sidebar card and a
    floating transparent header. One shared shell, two outcomes. (R-05.)
2. [MEDIUM] The transparent header has no surface, so it does not read
    as a workspace region and its top edge has no defined relationship
    with the sidebar top. (R-20.)
3. [LOW] The persistent global footer (pure branding text, no links)
    collides with the sidebar card's bottom edge and gives short and
    tall pages different bottom geometry. (R-05, C-3.)

## Approved fixes (per task directive)

- Aside `top-6` to `top-0` with matching `max-h`, square top corners so
  both tops align at rest and when stuck: sidebar top = header top.
- Header becomes a compact white surface (`rounded-b-xl`, border,
  `shadow-sm`), same 68px rhythm, all controls and behavior preserved.
- Footer renders for non-librarians only; bottom spacing stays on
  `main` (`py-6 sm:py-8`, i.e. 24-32px token).
- No page-template edits needed: title gaps already `mb-6` everywhere.
